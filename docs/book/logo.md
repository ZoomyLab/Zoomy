## Usage

- Use the logo unaltered: no recoloring, cropping, distortion or added effects.
- Do not use it in a way that implies endorsement by or affiliation with Zoomy or ZoomyLab.
- Do not use it as the logo of a fork or a derived product.
- Keep clear space around it.
