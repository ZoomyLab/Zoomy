SWE Title-Slide Generator

Resolution

NY is derived from NX × (H/W). 16:9 is the modern projector default.

Time

Frame dt = endTime / (nFrames−1). 900 frames over 30 s ≈ a 30 fps capture.

Openings

Fractions of the height (0=bottom, 1=top). Rest of the perimeter is wall (invisible).

Inflow

Piecewise-linear curve scaling q_in over time. Defaults to constant 1.
